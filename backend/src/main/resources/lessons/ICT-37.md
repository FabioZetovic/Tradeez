> The map of all the places price reacts: gaps, blocks, old highs and lows.

## What it is
The PD array matrix is ICT's list of every array price tends to react to — fair value gaps, order blocks, breakers, liquidity voids, old highs and lows — sorted into premium (above equilibrium) and discount (below it). It is a way of organising what is on the chart.

## How to spot it
- Mark the range and its equilibrium.
- List the arrays in premium and in discount.

## How it is traded
Look for longs only at discount arrays and shorts only at premium arrays.
