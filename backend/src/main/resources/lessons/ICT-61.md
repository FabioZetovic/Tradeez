> Before the day starts: is it more likely to go up or down?

## What it is
Daily bias is your expectation for the day's direction, formed before you trade. It comes from the higher timeframe narrative: where the daily chart is drawing to, which side's liquidity is closer, and how yesterday closed. A simple version: does today reach for yesterday's high or yesterday's low first?

## How to spot it
- Read the daily chart first: which way is it drawing?
- Mark yesterday's high and low and where today opens between them.
- Ask which one the market is more likely to take first, and why.

## How it is traded
Trade only in the direction of your bias. If the bias is bullish, look for longs at discount arrays after a sweep of sell-side; if bearish, shorts at premium arrays after a sweep of buy-side. If the day proves the bias wrong, stop trading it.
