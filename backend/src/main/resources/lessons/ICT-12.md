> Two highs (or lows) at almost the same price: a magnet for price, because the stops above them are obvious.

## What it is
Relative equal highs are two swing highs at nearly the same price; relative equal lows are two swing lows. Retail traders see them as double tops and bottoms — "support" and "resistance" — and put stops just beyond. ICT reads them the other way: an obvious pool of liquidity that price is likely to come back and take.

## How to spot it
- Two swing highs (or lows) within a few ticks of each other.
- The second one completes the pair.
- The cleaner and more obvious they look, the more stops are behind them.

## How it is traded
Do not buy equal lows or sell equal highs expecting them to hold. Use them as targets: when your bias points toward them, they are where price is likely to go. A sweep of equal highs or lows is a classic setup for a reversal.
