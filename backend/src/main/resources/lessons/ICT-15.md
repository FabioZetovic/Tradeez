> Price moves between the edges of the range and the gaps inside it.

## What it is
External range liquidity (ERL) is the liquidity at the edges of the current range — its swing high and swing low. Internal range liquidity (IRL) is inside it — mostly the fair value gaps. ICT's model of price is that it moves from one to the other: after it takes an external level, it tends to come back into an internal gap, and from an internal gap it tends to reach for the next external level.

## How to spot it
- Find the range: the swing high and swing low price is between.
- Its edges are ERL; the open fair value gaps inside it are IRL.
- Note which one price took last.

## How it is traded
After price sweeps an external level, look for the retrace into the internal gap as an entry, and target the external level on the other side. After price fills an internal gap, look for the move to the next external level.
