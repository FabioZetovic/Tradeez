> The shapes a day (or a week) tends to take.

## What it is
Daily profiles are ICT's templates for how a trading day unfolds — for example, a London reversal that New York continues, or a day that consolidates into New York and then expands. Weekly profiles do the same for the week: a low of the week formed on Monday or Tuesday in a bullish week, and so on.

## How to spot it
- Note where the day's high and low were made, and in which session.
- Compare the shape with the common profiles.

## How it is traded
Use the profiles to set expectations for when the day's move is likely to come, not as a signal.
