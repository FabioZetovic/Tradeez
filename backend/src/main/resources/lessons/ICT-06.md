> Did structure break with the trend or against it? Break of structure, change of character, market structure shift.

## What it is
When a candle closes beyond a swing, structure has broken. If the break goes with the trend — a close above a swing high in an uptrend — it is a break of structure (BOS): the trend continues. If it goes against the trend, it is a change of character (CHoCH): the first sign the trend may be turning. ICT calls the meaningful version of this a market structure shift (MSS): a swing is swept for its liquidity first, then price displaces and closes through the swing on the other side.

## How to spot it
- Find the latest swing high and swing low, and the trend they make.
- Wait for a candle to close beyond one of them — a wick does not count.
- With the trend: BOS. Against it: CHoCH. Against it after a sweep, with displacement: a market structure shift.

## How it is traded
A BOS confirms you should keep trading with the trend. A market structure shift is an entry trigger: after the shift, wait for price to come back into the fair value gap the displacement left, enter there, put the stop beyond the high or low that made the sweep, and target the liquidity on the other side.
