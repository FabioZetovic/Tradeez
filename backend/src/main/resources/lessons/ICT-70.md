> The model that ties it all together: sweep, shift, gap, target.

## What it is
ICT's 2022 model is a full sequence: inside a killzone, price sweeps liquidity, then shifts structure the other way with displacement (a market structure shift), leaving a fair value gap. You enter on the retrace into that gap, with the stop beyond the high or low that made the sweep, targeting the opposing liquidity.

## How to spot it
- Is it inside a killzone (London or New York)?
- Was liquidity swept, followed by a market structure shift with displacement?
- Did the shift leave a fair value gap, and has price come back into it?
- Is the stop beyond the high or low that made the sweep, and the target at resting liquidity on the other side?

## How it is traded
Every step must be there. Enter in the gap, stop beyond the sweep's extreme, target the opposing liquidity. If any step is missing, it is not the model — pass.
