> Where is price going next? To the liquidity it has not taken yet.

## What it is
The draw on liquidity is the pool price is heading for — the nearest untaken buy-side above or sell-side below that the market is likely to reach next. Some paths are easier than others: a path with few swing points and no open gaps against it is low resistance; a path full of old swings and gaps facing the move is high resistance.

## How to spot it
- Mark the nearest resting buy-side above price and sell-side below it.
- Count what is on the way to each: swing points and open gaps facing the move.
- The side with less in the way, and the side the higher timeframe points to, is the more likely draw.

## How it is traded
The draw on liquidity is your target and your bias in one. Only take trades toward it, and take profit at it. If price reaches the draw and is rejected, the next draw is usually the pool on the other side.
