> The gap between yesterday's 16:15 close and today's 09:30 open on the regular session.

## What it is
The opening range gap is the gap between the previous regular session's close and the new 09:30 open. Price frequently trades back into it early in the session, and ICT's opening range gap model trades exactly that: a move back into the gap, toward its middle, after the open.

## How to spot it
- Mark yesterday's 16:15 New York close and today's 09:30 open.
- The space between is the opening range gap.

## How it is traded
Watch the gap in the first hour: a move back toward its middle is common, and a reaction at its edges or middle can be a short-term entry.
