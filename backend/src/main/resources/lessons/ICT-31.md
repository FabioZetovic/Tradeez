> A swing that failed after a sweep: the candles that made it become a level the other way.

## What it is
A breaker block forms when price takes the liquidity beyond a swing, then displaces back through the swing on the other side. The candles that made the failed swing become the breaker: in a bullish breaker, the up candles at the high that broke; price often returns to them. A mitigation block is the same without the sweep — a failed swing broken without first running the liquidity.

## How to spot it
- A swing low, then a swing high; price goes below the first low (taking its liquidity).
- A displacement up closes above the swing high.
- The candles of that high are the bullish breaker. Mirror it for a bearish one.

## How it is traded
Enter when price returns to the breaker, in the direction of the displacement. The stop goes beyond the low or high that made the sweep; target the liquidity on the other side.
