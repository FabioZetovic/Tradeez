> One hour, one gap: the simplest complete ICT model.

## What it is
The Silver Bullet is a time-based model: inside one of three one-hour windows — 03:00, 10:00 or 14:00 New York — wait for a fair value gap to form in the direction of the draw on liquidity, enter when price retraces into it, and target the nearest liquidity at least ten points away.

## How to spot it
- Is it inside a Silver Bullet hour?
- Has a fair value gap formed in that hour, in the direction you expect?
- Has price come back into the gap?
- Is there resting liquidity at least ten points away to target?

## How it is traded
Enter in the gap during the hour. The stop goes beyond the gap — beyond the candle that started it. Take profit at the targeted liquidity. If no gap forms in the hour, there is no trade.
