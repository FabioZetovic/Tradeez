> When things happen: the day's opens, the sessions, the killzones, the Silver Bullet hours.

## What it is
ICT trades by the New York clock. The trading day opens at 18:00 and halts at 17:00; midnight is the open ICT measures the day from, 09:30 the stock market open. The sessions are Asia (evening), London (02:00–05:00) and New York (morning and afternoon). The killzones are the windows where the big moves usually happen: London 02:00–05:00, New York AM 08:30–11:00, New York PM 13:30–16:00. The Silver Bullet hours are 03:00, 10:00 and 14:00, an hour each, and macros are short windows inside them.

## How to spot it
- Midnight New York: the day's open price.
- London 02:00–05:00, New York AM 08:30–11:00, New York PM 13:30–16:00: the killzones.
- 03:00, 10:00, 14:00: the Silver Bullet hours.
- Switch the clock layers on in the Chart tab to see them on the chart.

## How it is traded
Do most of your trading inside a killzone, where the volume is, and be wary of setups outside them. The time windows do not give a trade on their own; they tell you when to look for one.
