> Split the range in half: buy in the lower half, sell in the upper half.

## What it is
Take the dealing range — the swing low and swing high of the latest move. Its midpoint is equilibrium. Above it is premium, where price is expensive; below it is discount, where price is cheap. ICT's rule is simple: look to buy in discount and sell in premium, never the other way round.

## How to spot it
- Find the latest move that broke structure: its low and its high are the range.
- Halfway between them is equilibrium.
- Is price above it (premium) or below it (discount)?

## How it is traded
Only take longs when price is in discount of the range and shorts when it is in premium. If your setup is a long but price sits in premium, wait for it to come down.
