> A swing high or low with long wicks: the wicks are the block.

## What it is
A rejection block is formed by the wicks of a swing high or low — long wicks showing price was rejected there. ICT reads the area between the bodies and the wick tips as a level price may return to and be rejected from again.

## How to spot it
- A swing high (or low) with long wicks.
- The area from the bodies to the wick tips is the block.

## How it is traded
Use it as a level where price may be rejected again, not as a stand-alone entry.
