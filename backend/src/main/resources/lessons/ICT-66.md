> NQ and ES should move together. When one makes a new high and the other does not, something is off.

## What it is
Smart money technique (SMT) divergence compares two related markets — NQ and ES. Normally both make new highs and new lows together. When one makes a higher high and the other a lower high (or one a lower low and the other a higher low), they diverge. ICT reads that as a crack: the move is losing support and may reverse.

## How to spot it
- Put NQ and ES side by side over the same minutes.
- At a swing, does one market go beyond its previous swing while the other does not?
- That disagreement is the divergence.

## How it is traded
Use SMT as confirmation, not as an entry on its own: a sweep of a high on NQ while ES fails to make a higher high supports a short; a sweep of a low with ES holding a higher low supports a long.
