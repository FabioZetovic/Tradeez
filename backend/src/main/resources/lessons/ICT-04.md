> The strong, fast move that shows real buyers or sellers stepped in.

## What it is
Displacement is a sharp move made of large candle bodies — larger than the candles before them — moving one way with little hesitation. It is the footprint of big orders. A move without displacement is just drift; a move with it is the market committing.

## How to spot it
- Look for one to three candles with bodies clearly bigger than the recent average.
- They close near their extremes, with small wicks against the move.
- A real displacement usually leaves a fair value gap behind it.

## How it is traded
Displacement is what makes the other patterns count: a structure break with displacement is a real shift, and the gap it leaves is where you look to enter on the retrace. No displacement, no trade.
