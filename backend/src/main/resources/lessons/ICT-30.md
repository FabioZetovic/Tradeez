> The last candle against the move, before a strong move that broke structure.

## What it is
An order block is the last candle (or run of candles) closing against the direction of a strong move — the last down candle before a displacement up that breaks a swing high, or the last up candle before a displacement down. ICT reads it as where the big orders were placed. Its middle is the mean threshold.

## How to spot it
- Find a displacement that breaks structure.
- Look back for the last candle that closed the other way before it — that is the block.
- Its body's middle is the mean threshold.

## How it is traded
When price comes back into the block, enter in the direction of the move that left it. The stop goes beyond the block (or its mean threshold, for a tighter stop); target the next liquidity. A block that price closes through has failed.
