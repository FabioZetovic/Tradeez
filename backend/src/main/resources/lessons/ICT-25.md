> Two candles in a row whose bodies do not touch, though their wicks do.

## What it is
A volume imbalance is a smaller cousin of the fair value gap: between two consecutive candles, the bodies leave a gap even though the wicks overlap. On NQ, where price moves fast, they are common, and price often comes back to close them.

## How to spot it
- Look at two candles side by side.
- The first candle's body ends and the second's begins at different prices, with a gap between.
- The wicks still overlap — if they did not, it would be a bigger gap.

## How it is traded
Treat a volume imbalance like a small fair value gap: a place price is likely to come back to, and a place to refine an entry inside a larger setup. On its own it is a weak reason to trade.
