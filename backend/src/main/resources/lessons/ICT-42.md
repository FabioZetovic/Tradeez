> The sweet spot of a pullback: 62% to 79% of the way back.

## What it is
The optimal trade entry (OTE) is a retracement zone: after a move, the area 62% to 79% of the way back toward where the move started, with 70.5% as its centre line. It is measured with ICT's Fibonacci levels. A pullback into the OTE is deep enough to be a real discount (or premium) without having undone the move.

## How to spot it
- Measure the leg from its start to its end.
- The zone from 62% to 79% of the way back from the end is the OTE; 70.5% is its line.
- After a leg up, the OTE is below price; after a leg down, above it.

## How it is traded
Wait for price to pull back into the OTE in the direction of the leg and enter there, ideally where it lines up with a fair value gap or order block. The stop goes beyond the start of the leg; targets are the leg's end and the liquidity beyond it.
