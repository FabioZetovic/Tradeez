> The gap between Friday's close and Sunday's open, and between one day's close and the next day's open.

## What it is
A new week opening gap (NWOG) is the price gap between Friday's 17:00 close and the Sunday 18:00 open. A new day opening gap (NDOG) is the gap between one day's 17:00 close and the next day's 18:00 open. ICT watches these levels for days or weeks: price often returns to them, and their middle acts as a reference.

## How to spot it
- Mark the last price before the close and the first price after the open.
- The space between is the opening gap.

## How it is traded
Use the opening gaps as reference levels and targets, not as entries on their own.
