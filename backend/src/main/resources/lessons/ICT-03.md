> Higher highs and higher lows, or lower highs and lower lows: which way the market is going.

## What it is
Market structure is the order of the swings. In an uptrend each swing high is higher than the last and each swing low is higher than the last — higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL). A downtrend is lower highs (LH) and lower lows (LL). That order is the market telling you who is in control.

## How to spot it
- Name each swing against the previous swing of its kind: is this high higher, lower or equal to the last high?
- HH and HL in a row: bullish order flow. LH and LL in a row: bearish.
- The first lower low in an uptrend (or higher high in a downtrend) is the warning that structure is changing.

## How it is traded
Trade with the structure: in bullish order flow look for longs at higher lows, in bearish order flow look for shorts at lower highs. When the order breaks, stop looking for continuation and wait for the new structure to show itself.
