> A bullish gap and a bearish gap overlapping: the overlap is a balanced price range.

## What it is
A balanced price range (BPR) forms when price leaves a fair value gap in one direction and then a fair value gap in the other direction over the same prices. The overlap has been imbalanced both ways, and ICT treats it as an especially strong area.

## How to spot it
- Find a fair value gap, then a later gap in the opposite direction.
- Where the two overlap in price is the BPR.

## How it is traded
Use it like a fair value gap: an area to enter on a retrace, in the direction of the latest move.
