> How far a move may run: the leg measured again, past its end.

## What it is
Standard deviation projections take a leg — often the manipulation leg of the day — and project multiples of it beyond the move (one, two, two and a half, four times). ICT uses them as targets: places where a move tends to slow or turn.

## How to spot it
- Measure the leg.
- Project it again from its end: −1, −2, −2.5, −4 standard deviations.

## How it is traded
Use the projections as profit targets and as places to stop expecting more, not as entries.
