> The trade is open and working. Hold, move the stop, or take some off?

## What it is
Trade management is what you do between entry and exit: hold to the plan, move the stop to breakeven, or take partial profit. Each has a cost: moving the stop too early gets you stopped out of good trades, holding everything gives back open profit when the trade turns. The right choice depends on where price is relative to the liquidity and structure ahead.

## How to spot it
- Where is price against your entry, in R?
- Is there liquidity or an opposing array between price and the target?
- Has structure shifted against you?

## How it is traded
Decide your management rules before the trade, then follow them. A common plan: take part off at the first liquidity, move the stop only once structure confirms the move, and hold the rest to the target.
