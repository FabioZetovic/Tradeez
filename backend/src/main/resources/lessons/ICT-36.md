> A breaker and a fair value gap on top of each other: two reasons in one zone.

## What it is
A unicorn is a breaker block overlapped by the fair value gap of the very displacement that formed it. The overlap is the zone. ICT treats it as a high-probability entry because two of his entry arrays agree on the same prices.

## How to spot it
- Find a breaker block.
- Find the fair value gap the breaker's displacement left.
- Where the two overlap in price is the unicorn.

## How it is traded
Enter on the retrace into the overlap, in the direction of the displacement. The stop goes beyond the high or low that made the sweep; target the next liquidity.
